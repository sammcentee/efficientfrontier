The simple rule
  1. Hold 75% in SPY.
  2. Rank stocks by their adjusted-price return over months 2–12, with the latest month excluded.
  3. Hold the top 50 at 0.5% each. Together, they form the other 25% of the portfolio.
  4. Use month-end signals. Reset the weights at the next trading close.

The available stocks come from current S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100 constituents with complete history. A past-price screen excludes long unchanged-price runs.
